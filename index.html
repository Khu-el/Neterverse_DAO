📜 Jurisdictional Notice

What the trust asserts. The Neterverse DAO is a private ecclesiastical entity established under Natural Law, Canon Law, and international equity. No presumed statutory authority applies unless by consent. All operations are ecclesiastical, private, and honor-based.

What that covers. The trust's own governance, records, and offices, and the people who join it by their own consent. Within that circle, the trust's instruments govern what they govern.

What it does not claim. This notice states the trust's own position; it is not a ruling on anyone else's. It does not claim that any agency, court, bank, or other outside party is bound by it without that party's own agreement or controlling law. Joining the DAO does not change a member's citizenship, tax, licensing, or other civil obligations, and no record the trust issues is a government document.

Nothing on this page is an offer to sell, or a solicitation to buy, any token, coin, membership interest, or other digital asset. Issuing one would be a separate decision requiring its own legal review.
